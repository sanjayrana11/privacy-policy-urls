# Privacy Policy for Bhakti Path: Panchang & Kundli

**Effective Date:** September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application **Bhakti Path- Panchang, Sadhana** (referred to as **"BhaktiPath"**, the **"App"**, **"we"**, **"our"**, or **"us"**), developed and published by **Sanjay Rana** on the Google Play Store.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you download and use our App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- We do **not** require account creation, registration, or login.
- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit personal data such as your name, phone number, email address, physical address, or contact list.

### B. Automatically Collected Information & Advertising
We use third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) to serve ads within the App. These third parties may automatically collect certain device and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies, including:
- Device type, manufacturer, and operating system
- IP address (used to infer approximate location for relevant ads)
- Google Advertising ID (AAID)

For more information on how Google processes this data, please review:
- [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google AdMob Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543?hl=en)

---

## 2. How We Use Information
Any information processed through third-party services is used solely to:
- Display personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.
- Monitor app performance, prevent fraud, and maintain app stability.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Usage
The App requests only necessary device permissions to support core features:
- **Notifications & Alarms (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM`):** Used strictly to trigger puja, aarti, and daily reminder alerts set by you.
- **Audio & Media Playback (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`):** Used to ensure continuous background playback when listening to aartis, mantras, and bhajans.
- **Set Wallpaper (`SET_WALLPAPER`):** Used exclusively when you choose to set devotional images as your phone wallpaper.

None of these permissions are used to collect or extract your personal information.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion
Since we do not operate user accounts or store personal user data on external servers, we do not retain your personal data. User preferences (such as reminder settings or saved content) are stored locally on your device and are permanently removed when you clear app data or uninstall the App.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
The App is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.
